About us

About SWS Trading Enterprises (Pvt) Ltd.

A Pakistan-based import and trading company focused on the sourcing and supply of feed ingredients, animal nutrition products and related raw materials.

We aim to bridge the gap between international suppliers and local buyers by providing professional import and sourcing solutions.

Successful trading is not simply about buying and selling. It is about reliable supply chains, professional relationships and delivering value to customers.

As our business grows, we are expanding our international sourcing network and product portfolio to meet the evolving needs of the feed and animal nutrition industry.
